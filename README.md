# Fida Technologies

**Enterprise Software, ERP, Web & Mobile Solutions in Afghanistan**

Fida Technologies is a software and technology company based in **Kabul, Afghanistan**, building practical digital systems for organizations with real operational, financial and reporting requirements.

We design and develop **ERP, MIS, accounting and financial systems, custom business software, web applications, professional websites, mobile applications, APIs and integrations**.

## What we build

- **ERP & MIS** — connected finance, HR, procurement, inventory, operations and reporting
- **Accounting & Financial Systems** — multi-currency finance, approvals, cash/bank, receivables and payables
- **Custom Business Software** — workflow-driven systems designed around each organization
- **Web Applications & Websites** — Laravel-based platforms, portals and professional multilingual websites
- **Mobile Applications** — Android and iOS applications with secure APIs and administration platforms
- **Business Automation & Integrations** — approvals, dashboards, reporting, APIs and system integrations
- **Industry Software** — solutions for finance and Sarafi, healthcare and pharmacy, education, construction, real estate, manufacturing, mining, logistics, retail and other operational sectors
